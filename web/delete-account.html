Delete your account

This public page explains how to delete your SideQuest account and what will be removed. You can also delete your account directly inside the app from Settings.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and username
  • Your posts, comments, likes, and messages
  • Associated account data kept for app operation

How to request deletion

  • Open the app and go to Settings > Delete account.
  • If you cannot access the app, email support@sidequest.app.
  • Include the email address of the account you want deleted.
Email support

Need help?

For questions about deletion or privacy, contact support@sidequest.app.
